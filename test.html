Nightlex · for testers

7-day test

Thank you for helping! For 7 days, use Nightlex for 10–20 minutes a day, then fill in the short form at the bottom of this page (5 minutes).

Nightlex is an app for Ukrainians learning American English or Danish: words repeated at the right moment, grammar, reading, dialogues and listening. The app has two interface languages: Ukrainian and English. If you don’t read Ukrainian, tap «UA» in the top-right corner of the start screen and choose English — you will then test the Danish course with English explanations. The button names below are given in Ukrainian with an English translation. Honest criticism helps us more than praise.

Words, sentences and translations were written by an AI and have not been checked by native speakers yet. If you spot a language mistake — that is exactly what we are looking for.

⚠️ Please read this whole page first (about 3 minutes) — only then open Nightlex and add it to your Home Screen. Everything is explained step by step below, so you won’t get lost.

How to start

  1. Open Nightlex on your phone — in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
  2. Add it to your Home Screen so it opens like a normal app and works offline:
    iPhone — Share → “Add to Home Screen”; Android — menu ⋮ → “Add to Home screen”. Step by step ↓
  3. Choose the language: English (US) or Danish. With the English interface there is only Danish.
  4. If you know the language a little — take the level test (10–12 min). Starting from zero — tap «Я зовсім новачок» (“I’m a complete beginner”).
  5. Choose a goal and start “Day 1”. Turn the sound on — words are read aloud.
Open Nightlex

📱 Put Nightlex on your Home Screen

Do this once — then Nightlex opens with one tap from an icon, full screen, like a normal app. No need to look for the link again.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open the Nightlex link in Safari (not inside Messenger, Instagram or Gmail — there, tap “Open in Safari” first).
  2. Tap the Share button: the square with an arrow pointing up (at the bottom of the screen, or next to the address bar).
  3. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”.
  4. Tap “Add” in the top-right corner. The Nightlex icon appears on your Home Screen.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the Nightlex link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu ⋮ (three dots, top right).
  3. Tap “Add to Home screen” (or “Install app”), then “Add”.

From now on, open Nightlex from the icon. Your progress stays on the phone either way.

🧭 How to test

  • Play like a normal learner. Do the daily plan, don’t rush, don’t reset your progress.
  • Always open Nightlex the same way — from the Home Screen icon. Progress is saved only on this phone; don’t clear Safari/Chrome data.
  • Something wrong? Take a screenshot right away. A mistake in the language, an unclear button, a wrongly marked answer, a frozen screen — everything counts.
  • Write down short notes — one or two sentences: what you did and what you noticed (e.g. in your phone’s Notes app).
  • Send your notes and screenshots to the person who gave you the link (Telegram, Messenger, e-mail) — once a day or at least on day 7.
  • Day 7: in Nightlex open «Я» (“Me”) → «Звіт для тесту» (“Test report”) and send it, then fill in the form at the bottom of this page.

Are you a native Danish or English speaker?

Then the most useful thing you can do is check the language: a separate page shows words and sentences, and you mark what sounds natural and what does not. Even 10 minutes helps a lot.

Want to see the whole app at once, without waiting for the daily plan? In Nightlex open «Я» (“Me”) → «Налаштування» (“Settings”) → «Додатково» (“More”) and turn on “Open access to exercises”. Learners: please keep the normal flow — we want to see how it works day by day.

Pass it on. Checked, say, 100 words and want someone else to continue? 1) Tap “Save & send” → “Share” and send the file to the next person. 2) They open the same check page, write their name, tap 📂 “Continue where someone else stopped (open file)”, choose the file and tap Start — the words you already checked are skipped. Each person sends their own file at the end.
