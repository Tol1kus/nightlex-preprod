Nightlex · for testers

7-day test

Thank you for helping! For 7 days, use Nightlex for 10–20 minutes a day, then fill in the short form at the bottom of this page (5 minutes).

Nightlex is an app for Ukrainians learning American English or Danish: words repeated at the right moment, grammar, reading, dialogues and listening. The app itself is in Ukrainian — the button names below are given in Ukrainian with a translation. Honest criticism helps us more than praise.

Words, sentences and translations were written by an AI and have not been checked by native speakers yet. If you spot a language mistake — that is exactly what we are looking for.

Open Nightlex

How to start

  1. Open tol1kus.github.io/nightlex on your phone — in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
  2. Add it to your Home Screen so it opens like a normal app and works offline:
    iPhone — Share → “Add to Home Screen”; Android — menu ⋮ → “Add to Home screen”.
  3. Choose the language: English (US) or Danish.
  4. If you know the language a little — take the level test (10–12 min). Starting from zero — tap «Я зовсім новачок» (“I’m a complete beginner”).
  5. Choose a goal and start “Day 1”. Turn the sound on — words are read aloud.

The 7-day plan

Every day — the daily plan on the «Сьогодні» (“Today”) tab (button «Почати», “Start”), then one extra task. Missed a day — no problem, just continue.

The daily plan and the form belowDo you want to keep going?

If something goes wrong

Take a screenshot and send it to the person who gave you the link, with one sentence: what you were doing and what went wrong. We are especially interested in language mistakes, places where it is unclear what to tap, answers marked wrongly, and screens that froze or did not fit.

Your data

All progress is stored only on your phone: no sign-up, no ads, no analytics. We don’t see your answers — only what you send yourself. A copy of your progress and deleting it: «Налаштування» → «Дані» → «Приватність» (“Settings” → “Data” → “Privacy”).
