7-day test

Thank you for helping! Use Nightlex for 10–20 minutes a day for 7 days, and on day 7 fill in the short form at the bottom of this page.

Words and translations were written by an AI and have not been checked by native speakers yet. If you spot a mistake — that is exactly what we are looking for. Honest criticism helps more than praise.

📖 First read this whole page to the end (3–4 minutes), and only then get started: open Nightlex, add the icon, start checking. That way you won’t miss anything or get lost.

Are you a native Danish or English speaker?

Then start here: check the language first — the page shows words and sentences, and you mark what sounds natural. Your answers are saved for us automatically. Even 10 minutes helps a lot. The app itself (steps below) — afterwards, if you have time.

👥 Forward the link to native-speaker friends. Each person checks on their own phone, the page gives everyone a different level, and every person’s answers are saved separately under their name.

📱 Icon on your Home Screen

Open your check page (button above) and add it to your Home Screen — then it opens with one tap:

  • iPhone (Safari): Share ⬆︎ → “Add to Home Screen” → “Add”.
  • Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ → “Add to Home screen” → “Add”.

So that people don’t all check the same thing, the page suggests the level checked least so far (marked ★). Lines already checked by two other people are skipped.

💾 How your answers are saved

  • On your phone — after every word. Close it and open it again, and you continue where you stopped. Always open it from the same icon.
  • On our server — automatically every 10 answers, and again when you tap “Send”. You don’t need to send anything yourself.
  • No internet? Your answers wait on the phone; “Send” has a backup button — save a file.

Want to see the whole app without waiting for the daily plan: “Me” → “Settings” → “More” → “Open access to exercises”.

Open Nightlex

2. First start

  1. Interface language — choose English (Ukrainian speakers: Українська).
  2. Choose what you learn: Danish (with the Ukrainian interface also English US).
  3. Know a little — take the level test (10 min). From zero — “I’m a complete beginner”.
  4. Turn the sound on — words are read aloud.

3. Every day, 10–20 minutes

📷 Noticed something odd? Send a screenshot

Everything counts: a language mistake, an unclear button, a wrongly marked answer, a screen that froze or didn’t fit.

How to take a screenshot: iPhone — side button + volume up (at the same time); Android — power + volume down.

  1. Tap “Choose screenshot” and pick it from your photos.
  2. Write one sentence: what you did and what went wrong.
  3. Tap “Send screenshot” — it reaches us right away. Up to 25 screenshots a day from one phone.

You can also do it right in Nightlex: “Me” → “📷 Report a problem”. Don’t capture private things (messages, passwords).

4. Day 7

  1. In Nightlex: “Me” → “Test report”. The report is saved for us automatically.
  2. Fill in the form below and tap “Send answers”.
